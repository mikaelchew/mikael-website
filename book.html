Questions about the book

Is this book for beginners or experienced leaders?

Both. The 13 chapters move from foundations (finding your 'why', prospecting, inviting) to advanced leadership and duplication systems. New builders avoid years of costly mistakes; senior leaders get frameworks to build organizations that run without them.

What makes this different from other direct selling books?

Two things. It's written from a rare dual perspective (8 years in the field and 15 in corporate management), and it applies Sun Tzu's Art of War as a genuine strategic framework, not a gimmick. Every chapter pairs a principle with real stories, specific numbers, and a weekly exercise.

Will the strategies work outside Malaysia or Asia?

Yes. The stories are drawn from Southeast Asia, but the frameworks (strategic prospecting, the follow-up system, the golden 72 hours, ethical duplication) are based on human dynamics that hold across markets and companies.

Is the book tied to a specific company?

No. The book is deliberately company-agnostic. It's about how to think and lead strategically in direct selling, whatever company you build with. Endorsers are credited with their own names, titles and organisations, for identification only; the book itself promotes no company.
