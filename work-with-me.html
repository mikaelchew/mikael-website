FAQ

Before You Reach Out

Who is this mentorship right for?

Serious, committed leaders who have been in direct selling for at least two years and want strategy — not another motivation speech. If you're willing to do the work and build with integrity, we'll get along. If you're looking for a magic script or a shortcut, I'm not your guy.

Do I have to join your company?

No. My mentorship is deliberately company-agnostic. I'm not recruiting you into anything — I help you think and lead more strategically wherever you already build. The frameworks work across companies and compensation plans.

What does working together actually look like?

We start with a conversation about where you are and what's blocking you. From there it's structured, strategic sessions — diagnosing blind spots from both the field and corporate angles, building a clear plan for your market and team, and reviewing progress. It's mentorship, not a course you watch alone.

Can you guarantee I'll hit a certain rank or income?

No — and be careful with anyone who does. I can give you the strategy, the frameworks, and honest feedback, but the results depend on your work, your market, and your consistency. What I promise is clarity and a plan, not a number.
