This policy applies to the ebook 《直銷孫子兵法之不戰而勝》 (EPUB + PDF) bought directly on mikaelchew.com. Copies bought on Amazon Kindle follow Amazon’s own refund policy.

Delivery

Your ebook is emailed to the address you enter, usually within minutes of payment. If it hasn’t arrived within 24 hours (please check your spam folder), email mikaelchew@gmail.com with your name and payment receipt and we will resend it.

When we refund in full

• You paid but did not receive the book, and we could not deliver it within 3 working days of you telling us
• You were charged twice for the same order
• The file is damaged or will not open, and a replacement does not fix it

Change of mind

Because the ebook is a digital file delivered immediately, we don’t usually refund a change of mind. If the book isn’t what you expected, write to us within 7 days of purchase and tell us why. We look at every request individually.

How to ask for a refund

Email mikaelchew@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase with your receipt (Billplz bill ID). Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days.
